## Description
Every environment, once migrated, holds the same reference data: the six trades workers can offer, the placeholder Belgrade area tree (city → municipality → neighborhood) and the partner store Home Center Vračar with referral code `VRACAR01`. Anyone, signed in or not, can list the trades and browse the areas one level at a time, which is what the trade picker, the area picker and the new-request form will use.

## Stories
- US-0018 Reference data is in place in every environment
- US-0019 Anyone can list the trades
- US-0020 Anyone can browse areas level by level

## Business rules
- Areas in three levels: city → municipality → neighborhood; jobs and worker coverage point at neighborhoods; seeded by hand for the pilot city (workflow.md section 1, Location).
- `GET /trades` and `GET /areas?parentId=` are open to anyone; areas load level by level (workflow.md section 4).
- Placeholder Belgrade areas until the pilot city is chosen; seeded store Home Center Vračar, `VRACAR01` (workflow.md section 7, "Seeded development data").
- MVP focus is appliances, electrical and plumbing; painting and carpentry are allowed trades but not the focus (workflow.md section 1, MVP scope).
