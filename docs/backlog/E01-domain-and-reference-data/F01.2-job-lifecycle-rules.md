## Description
A job can only move along the lifecycle in workflow.md section 2: Open → Assigned (client hires a bid) → Done (either side finishes) → Closed (both have rated), with Cancelled reachable from Open or Assigned. Every other move is refused by the domain, whatever endpoint later calls it. Hiring settles the bids (one accepted, the other active ones rejected), and a job closes only when both the client and the hired worker have rated.

## Stories
- US-0013 A job moves only along the allowed lifecycle
- US-0014 A job closes only when both sides have rated

## Business rules
- Job lifecycle and who can act in each status (workflow.md section 2, Job lifecycle).
- State transitions are `Job` methods (`Hire`, `Finish`, `Cancel`, `AddRating`) that refuse invalid transitions with a `DomainException`, mapped to `409` (workflow.md section 5, Backend conventions).
- Ratings are 1–5 stars with an optional comment; one per side (workflow.md section 2, Step 7; section 3).
